# The Tutor View

As a tutor you review what students hand in: you look at their work, test it,
grade it, and tell them what to change. Computor gives you two views for that
in the sidebar:

- **Courses & Members** — where you pick the course and the student to review.
- **Courses** — that student's course content, with everything you need to
  inspect, test and grade an assignment.

This page explains finding your way around and finding the work that is
waiting for you. [Grading an assignment](tutor-assignment.md) covers the work
on a single assignment.

## Picking a student

**Courses & Members** lists your courses under their organization and course
family. Click a course to select it, then:

- **Course Groups** narrows the member list to one group. Pick *All groups* to
  see everyone, *No Group* for members without one.
- Click a member to load their content into the **Courses** view.

Two badges behind a member's name tell you where work is waiting:

| Badge | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 📝 3 | Three submitted assignments you have not reviewed yet |
| 🔔 2 | Two unread messages from this student |

Work through the members with a 📝 badge and it disappears as you grade —
that badge *is* your to-do list.

## Reading the content tree

The **Courses** view starts with three rows repeating your selection — course,
group, member — followed by the units and assignments of the course, in the
order students see them.

**Units** show how many items they contain, and carry the same `[📝]` and
`[🔔]` markers as their assignments, so a collapsed unit never hides waiting
work.

**Assignments** are the rows you act on. The icon's corner shows the grading
status you set (green for corrected, red for correction necessary, amber for
improvement possible); the badge on it shows the student's last test result
and whether they submitted. The numbers behind the name are the student's
budgets — `(2/5)` means two test runs used of five allowed, and the second
pair counts submissions — followed by the test result and the grade as
percentages. Hover for the same information in words, including who graded it.

Clicking an assignment downloads the student's latest submission and the
reference solution, and opens the test results if there are any. See
[Grading an assignment](tutor-assignment.md).

## Hidden content

Rows marked `👁 invisible` are content the lecturer has hidden from students —
usually staged material or a closed exam. You still see these rows, and you can
still grade them: an exam is often hidden right after it ends, and its
submissions still need marks.

## Messages and comments

**Show Messages** on an assignment opens your conversation with the student
about that assignment — this is where you tell them what to fix. When you set a
grading status of *Correction Necessary* or *Improvement Possible*, this
conversation opens by itself, because the student cannot act on the verdict
until you say what to change.

**Show Comments** on a member is different: comments are notes about the
student that only course staff can read. The student never sees them. Use them
for anything the next tutor or the lecturer should know.
